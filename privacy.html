Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026.

This website is a static information and enquiry site. It does not collect or upload your pet photos on the page. When you select the order button, your own email application opens with a draft. Your information is only sent when you choose to send that email.

Information you choose to share

Enquiry emails may contain your name, email address, pet name, description and any photos you attach. This information is used to respond to your enquiry and, if you agree to proceed, fulfil your requested artwork. Please do not send confidential information.

Hosting and external services

The hosting provider may process technical information to serve this website. There is currently no integrated payment service, file-upload backend or visitor analytics script on this site.

Keeping and deleting enquiries

Messages may remain in email records as needed to manage enquiries and orders. You can request deletion of the information you have sent, subject to applicable legal recordkeeping requirements.
