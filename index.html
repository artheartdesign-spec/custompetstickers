Questions? Answered.

Can you make a custom sticker of my dog or cat from a photo?

Yes. Send us a clear photo and a few details about your pet. We create a personalised chibi-style illustration inspired by their distinctive features and personality.

Are these physical stickers or digital files?

The initial offer is digital artwork. Physical printed stickers are not included. You'll receive exact delivery details and pricing in your quote.

How do I send a photo of my pet?

Complete the enquiry form and add your photo as an attachment in the email draft it opens. Your image is not uploaded to this website.

Can I request different expressions?

Absolutely. Tell us whether your pet is cheeky, sleepy, dramatic, energetic or something completely different. We'll confirm what is included in your quote.
